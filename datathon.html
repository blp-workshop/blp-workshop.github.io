Third Workshop on Bangla Language Processing

Task 3: BLP Datathon 2027

The BLP Datathon 2027 asks volunteer teams to record regional Bangla speech, so that we can find where open-source speech-to-text (ASR) models fail. Most Bangla ASR models learn from standard Bangla, so they probably make many more errors on regional speech, for example from Sylhet, Chattogram, Noakhali, Rangpur or Barishal. Teams record volunteer speakers, type the exact words that they say, and help build a public dataset of regional Bangla. You do not need research experience. All details are on the datathon website.
