## Call for Sponsors: BLP 2027

The **Third Workshop on Bangla Language Processing (BLP 2027)** invites companies and institutions to support research and community building in Bangla language technology. Sponsorship offers an opportunity to connect with researchers, industry experts, and postgraduate students from around the world.

### Why Sponsor BLP 2027?

- **Brand Visibility:** Showcase your organization to a global audience in academia and industry.
- **Talent Engagement:** Connect directly with emerging talent and researchers in Bangla language technology.
- **Community Impact:** Support the development of NLP technologies and language resources for Bangla and related languages.
- **Recognition:** Receive prominent recognition on our website, in the workshop program, and across workshop materials.

> **In 2023, our sponsors enabled us to award 21 grants** recognizing outstanding contributions, including Best Paper, Best Shared Task Paper, and Best Reviewer awards. **In 2025, we supported 15 authors.** This support included free registrations, compute credits, and other benefits.

### How to Sponsor

Interested in supporting BLP 2027? Please contact our Sponsorship Chair:

- **Dr. Shammur Absar Chowdhury:** [shchowdhury@hbku.edu.qa](mailto:shchowdhury@hbku.edu.qa)


Download the [BLP 2027 sponsorship brochure]({{ '/assets/Sponsorship_Brochure-BLP2027.pdf' | relative_url }}).

> To maximize the visibility and benefits of your sponsorship, please contact us as early as possible and no later than **January 30, 2027**.
